Proposal files are lightweight idea records, one proposal per file. They are
not specifications, commitments, scheduling authority, or permission to modify
the product. Keep each file small; link to evidence or a deeper review instead
of copying it.

A proposal normally needs investigation and a formalized owner decision before
promotion. The proposal directory is not the exclusive promotion path: a
finding, confirmed issue, audit, external-consumer record, or direct owner
decision may also produce promoted work when its direction is formalized and
accepted in [`TODO.md`](../../TODO.md).

When a proposal is promoted, rejected, superseded, or split, preserve the file,
update its state, and link the outcome. Do not silently delete its provenance.
